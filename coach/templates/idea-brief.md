# Idea Brief

Fill this in with your Hackathon Idea Coach during or after your session. Leave
any field `Unknown` rather than guessing — an honest gap is more useful than a
made-up answer.

Evidence mode: [ ] Real fieldwork [ ] Synthetic workshop roleplay

## Target person

Who specifically experiences this problem?

## Recent problem

Describe the last time it happened. Is this account:
- [ ] Your own direct experience
- [ ] Something you directly observed
- [ ] A secondhand report (someone told you)
- [ ] A hypothesis / guess you haven't confirmed

## Current behavior / workaround

What does this person do about the problem today?

## Reachable group

What specific, bounded group can you actually reach (course section, student
org, department, campus service, local business category, etc.)? Not "the
whole campus."

## Evidence gathered so far

What have you actually confirmed, from whom, and how?

# Experiment Card

One hackathon-sized experiment, sized to your team's actual time, skills, and
access — not an idealized team. The success criterion must be written down
**before** you run the test, not chosen afterward to match the result.

Evidence mode: [ ] Real fieldwork [ ] Synthetic workshop roleplay
Result: Not run (replace only with an explicitly reported result).

## Participants

Who takes part in this experiment? (Reference the reachable group from your
Idea Brief / Count Ledger.)

## What they do

Describe the concrete activity — what you ask them to do, use, or react to.

## What is observed

What specifically will you watch, measure, or record? Be concrete enough that
two different people watching would agree on what happened.

## Success criterion (set before the test)

Write the specific, falsifiable bar for success — a criterion that could
plausibly come back as "no." Avoid vague language like "positive feedback."

## What we change if the result disappoints

If the observed result doesn't meet the success criterion, what will you
actually do — narrow the idea, pivot, or investigate further? Decide this now,
not after seeing the result.
